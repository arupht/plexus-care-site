Five modules on one clinical spine.

Each reads a different signal and returns the same shape of answer: a band, the reasons, and what it is not sure about. What follows is what each does today, including what is unfinished.

ED Arrival Risk

Clinician pilot

A triage nurse has two minutes and incomplete information. This returns an attention band with the reasoning written out.

The nurse owns the final acuity. The CTAS level is used internally for ordering; the clinician sees the plain-English band and the target review time.

Chest X-ray

Clinic pilot

Three independent readers score the same film in parallel. Where they disagree, the disagreement is shown rather than smoothed over.

The confidence figure is a weighted ensemble-agreement score, not a calibrated probability of disease. Making it one requires fitting against labelled outcomes.

ECG

Models in training

The output is not a diagnosis. It is how fast a human must look, how confident we are, and where the evidence conflicts.

The CNN ensemble is not trained yet. Until it is, this path runs on the rule engine and the local model panel alone — and every report says so on its face.

Fusion
CNN ensemble 0.50, rule engine 0.35, local model panel 0.15
Asymmetric
Any voice may raise the tier. None may lower it below a confident rule finding.
Input
Waveforms, not images. Cart exports are exact; printouts are digitised first.
Refusal
A standard printout prints lead II twice. Comparing the two digitisations tells us whether the scan is trustworthy — and if it is not, the system declines to interpret.
Training data
PTB-XL: 21,799 cardiologist-annotated 12-lead ECGs

Head CT

Research only

An offline evaluation harness for intracranial haemorrhage, and a segmentation pipeline for quantifying it.

Evaluates
A vision model against the RSNA haemorrhage set, entirely offline
Reports
Sensitivity, specificity, Cohen's kappa, PPV and NPV — per image and per exam
Segmentation
Dense binary masks; bounding-box datasets are treated as boxes, not as truth

Research infrastructure, not a product. It must not be used for patient-care decisions, and it is not offered for sale.

Clinic OS

Foundation shipped

A multi-tenant, ABDM-compliant EMR for small clinics in tier-2 and tier-3 India.

What exists is the foundation, with no clinical features yet. It has the machinery every later module depends on and which cannot be retrofitted.

Isolation
PostgreSQL row-level security on the facility, not application filtering
Audit
Every access to patient data writes an event. There is no internal read.
Immutability
Signed documents are never updated; a correction creates a new version
Signature
No note, prescription or order is final without an explicit clinician signature
Drug safety
Deterministic rule engines, never a model call. Models may propose; rules decide.
Evidence
Every AI-extracted fact carries character offsets into its source, enforced by the schema
