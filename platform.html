Platform

A unified platform for frontline clinical decisions.

Plexus Care connects acute care, diagnostic signals and clinical operations through one coherent intelligence layer—built for clarity, control and scale.

01 · Acute care

Focus attention where it matters most.

Structures early clinical information into clear priorities, supporting faster recognition and more consistent escalation.

  • Clear, interpretable decision context
  • Consistent prioritisation across care teams
  • Human authority preserved at every decision
02 · Diagnostic imaging

Turn image review into a more focused workflow.

Brings relevant findings and uncertainty into view, helping clinical teams direct attention and communicate with greater consistency.

  • Prioritised review across imaging workflows
  • Structured context for clinical interpretation
  • Support for consistent reporting and follow-through
03 · Cardiac signals

Make complex signals easier to act on.

Organises physiological information into timely, interpretable guidance while keeping clinical judgment in command.

  • Structured signal interpretation
  • Visible confidence and uncertainty
  • Decision support aligned to clinical urgency
04 · Clinical operations

Connect decisions across the care journey.

Provides a secure, interoperable foundation for coordinated workflows, longitudinal context and accountable clinical action.

  • Connected records and decision context
  • Governed access and traceable actions
  • Flexible integration with modern care environments
