About

Named after the place where signals meet.

Plexus Care works on the first minutes of care — the point where the most is decided and the least is known.

The name

Plexus

Latin: a braid, a plaiting, an interweaving.

In anatomy, a plexus is a network where nerves arriving from several origins converge, interlace, and separate again as branches heading somewhere specific — the brachial plexus at the shoulder, the cardiac plexus at the base of the heart.

  • Nothing is invented there. A plexus generates no new signal; it reorganises what arrived.
  • The convergence is the function. Fibres from separate roots become useful only once combined.
  • Everything leaves addressed. The output is routed to a specific branch, not summarised.

A patient arrives carrying a story, a set of vitals, a film, ten seconds of ECG and a folder of old paper. Read separately they are five queues. Read together they are one decision.

How we work

The clinician sets the rules

Thresholds and wording live in configuration a doctor edits directly, rather than in code we argue about later.

Start where integration is not required

A tablet in an occupational health centre teaches us more, faster, than an eighteen-month hospital IT project.

Ship the boring foundation first

Tenant isolation, audit trails and immutability cannot be retrofitted, so they were built before any clinical feature.

Measure before believing

Several design decisions exist because a measurement contradicted a reasonable assumption.

Say what is not finished

Every page names the incomplete parts. Clinicians are the buyers, and they detect overstatement permanently.

Offline is the default

If it needs the cloud to read a study, it does not work where it is needed most.
