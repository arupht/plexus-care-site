How it decides

Rules decide. Models advise. Clinicians sign.

Rules decide

Guideline thresholds in a file a clinician can read and edit. Every finding names the criterion it came from.

Models advise

Independent readers score the same study. They may raise the urgency. They can never lower it.

Clinicians sign

Nothing is final without a signature. Where the layers disagree, the disagreement is shown rather than averaged away.

Where the data sits

Patient data does not leave the clinic to be useful.

The cloud is coordination, never the patient. Identifiers are stripped on-premise; the name is resolved in the radiologist's browser over the clinic LAN and is never written back.
