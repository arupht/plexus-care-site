The opportunity

Care is rich in data—and constrained by disconnected decisions.

Critical information often arrives across separate systems, formats and moments. PlexusCare creates a cohesive decision layer across the care journey, turning complexity into focused clinical action.

The result is a stronger operating model: more consistent decisions, greater workflow capacity and a platform that can extend across care settings.
